FAQ

Where do you deliver?

DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia: Washington, DC; Montgomery County and Prince George's County, MD; and Northern Virginia, including Tysons, Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax, and south to Lorton.

What dumpster sizes do you have?

10, 20, 30 and 40 yard roll-off dumpsters.

10 yard: smaller cleanouts and projects with limited space. 20 yard: remodeling debris, household cleanouts, and medium-size jobs. 30 yard: larger renovations, property cleanups, and active job sites. 40 yard: major cleanouts, commercial work, and bulky material.

Not sure? Tell us what you are clearing and we'll help match the container to the job.

How fast can you drop off a dumpster?

Same-day drop-off when you call before noon.

What can't go in the dumpster?

No tires and no mattresses in dumpsters.

Concrete, dirt and asphalt are charged at a flat rate, so call for that quote.

Not sure about an item? Call 202-450-5589 before you load it.

Who pulls the DC street permit?

For a dumpster on a DC street, you'll pull your own DC street permit.

What services do you offer?

Junk and debris removal, demolition, dumpster rental, dumpster sales, appliance and furniture removal, concrete and asphalt removal, flatbed, tilt bed and equipment hauling, and bulk trash pickup.

What comes with a rental?

Delivery and pickup are included: we drop it off and haul it away. A weight allowance comes with every rental, and you keep the dumpster for the full rental period at no extra charge.

How do I get a quote?

Call 202-450-5589, or send the Get a Quote form with your name, your phone number and what you need. We'll call you shortly.

Yes. Call or request a quote with the size and quantity you need (10, 20, 30 or 40 yard, single-container or multi-container) and Haul It All will give you a sales quote.
